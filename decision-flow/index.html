before you ask

The questions you're about to ask.

Will it work with my theme and apps?
It's a set of OS 2.0 sections, not a theme. It sits on top of what you have and works with most themes and common apps. Heavy custom logic may need minor styling adjustments; that's what the 14-day installation support covers.
Could it break my store?
It only touches the product page. Nothing near checkout, payments or app integrations. Because the sections sit alongside your theme, you can switch back to your old template anytime.
Do I need to code?
No. You add sections and adjust settings in the theme editor. If you can add a theme section, you can install this, and the setup guide walks through it screen by screen.
What if my conversion doesn't move?
Honest answer: this fixes how the product page behaves at the variant click, the disappearing button and the reopening cart. It can't create demand or fix a weak offer. If add-to-carts already happen and checkouts are inconsistent, this is built for exactly that gap.
Why trust it before there are reviews?
astriva is a solo build and Decision-Flow is its first product, so no wall of testimonials, and I won't manufacture one. What there is: eight published breakdowns of where product pages lose buyers, and a live demo you can click through yourself.
