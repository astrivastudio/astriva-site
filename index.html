01where stores lose

The buyer was ready. Nothing looked broken. They left anyway.

A store can be well-photographed, fast, and finished, and still lose buyers who were already convinced. The loss never shows up as an error. It shows up as a buyer who was moving toward purchase, and then wasn't.

Buying is not a single click. It is a decision carried through options, proof, price, trust, and action. A store can weaken that decision without ever looking broken.

When the page asks for more confidence than it gives back, a ready buyer quietly stops.

That loss is structural, not visual. It lives in how the store carries the decision, not only in how the store looks. That is what astriva builds for.

02the field

A store loses buyers in more places than it thinks.

Stores do not lose buyers at one fixed point. They lose them in small moments where intent is still present, but the page stops carrying the decision cleanly. astriva studies those moments without reducing the store to a funnel map.
